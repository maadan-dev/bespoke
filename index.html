    About Us

    The House of Bespoke by Yeni

    At Bespoke by Yeni, every bridal commission begins with your vision and proportions. Whether you bring a photograph you love or start from a fresh sketch during consultation, we adapt every neckline, corset structure, sleeve, and train to make the final gown unmistakably yours.

    From white wedding gowns and traditional ceremony attire to civil dresses, reception looks, bridesmaids, and coordinated aso ebi, our atelier is dedicated to creating elegant outfits you deserve.

    Custom Patterning

    Drafted individually to your exact measurements and posture.

    Couture Fabrics

    Curated Chantilly & guipure laces, duchess satin, illusion tulle, and hand beadwork.

    Two Fittings

    Dedicated fittings to perfect the internal corsetry, shape, and length.

    Steamed & Packed

    Delivered professionally steamed, pressed, and packed for your wedding day.
